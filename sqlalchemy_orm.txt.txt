EMBEDDINGS — SEMANTIC REPRESENTATION

Embeddings convert text into numerical vectors that capture meaning rather than exact wording. Two sentences with similar meaning produce vectors that are close together in vector space.

Cosine similarity measures how close two vectors are. It’s the backbone of semantic search: queries are embedded, compared to document embeddings, and ranked by similarity.

Chunking long documents into smaller pieces improves retrieval accuracy. Paragraph‑based chunking often preserves meaning better than fixed‑size chunking.
